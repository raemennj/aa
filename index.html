Roseville 5th Street Fellowship History

The Roseville 5th Street Fellowship is actually a splinter group of the Roseville Tuesday Night Group.

When the Tuesday night group moved to the Lincoln Street location in 1991, a small group of members remained at the 5th Street location to start the new group.

The first meeting of the Roseville 5th Street Fellowship was on April 28, 1991.

There were only a handful of people that attended that first meeting.

There were no Big Books in the room, no desk, no “Steps” or “Traditions” on the wall.

One of the members got a Big Book out of his car and the Roseville 5th Street Fellowship had their first meeting of Alcoholics Anonymous.

Thirty-five years later, the Roseville 5th Street Fellowship holds eighteen meetings each week.

We also host 24-hour Alcathons on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year’s, and a speaker meeting three times a year.
